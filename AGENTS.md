# Project instructions

- Do not use emoji characters in code, interface labels, documentation icons, or
  generated assets.
- Keep generated output under `output/`.
- Keep caches, smoke-test media, temporary files, and other disposable material
  under `runtime/`, except for the isolated `.venv/` environment.
- Preserve identity-first behavior. An uncertain observation must never be
  promoted to confirmed tracking merely to maintain continuity.
- Do not add weapon control, aiming, firing, or engagement functionality.
